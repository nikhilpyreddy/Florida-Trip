Emerald Coast

Destin beach day

Arrive around 9–10 AM and go straight to the sand. Sugar-white beach, clear green-blue water. Do the bulk of your relaxing by mid-afternoon so the evening drive isn't rough on no sleep.

Where to go in Destin

  • 1
    Henderson Beach State Park — the cleanest, least crowded stretch of white sand in Destin. $6 per car. This is the one to settle in at for the day.
  • 2
    Destin Harbor Boardwalk — open 24 hrs. Shops, food, harbor views, and the launch point for dolphin cruises and jet ski rentals.
  • 3
    HarborWalk Village — lively waterfront hub for dinner and drinks. Parking runs about $20.
  • 4
    Gator Beach — free alligator shows and hold-a-baby-gator photos. Quick, odd, fun break from the beach.
Destin → Lake City · 3.5 hrs on I-10 · check in late evening

Saturday means beaches and beach-town lodging fill up. Book the Lake City hotel ahead of time.

Freshwater springs

Ginnie Springs, all day

Tubing the Santa Fe River, snorkeling, and swimming in water that sits at 72°F year-round. Privately owned, roughly $25 per adult, with tube, mask, and paddleboard rentals on site.

Good to know

A new alcohol and open-container policy took effect in April 2026, so expect it calmer than the party reputation it used to have. Bring water shoes and a dry bag.

If you'd rather have quiet over busy

  • A
    Ichetucknee Springs — widely called the best tubing run in Florida. State park, so more rules and fewer rentals, and it fills up on weekends.
  • B
    Gilchrist Blue Springs — right next door to Ginnie. Quieter, cheaper, excellent snorkeling.

Epic Universe

Ride order

  • With Early Park Admission, be at the gate by 8:15 AM. Without it, 9:15 AM for the 10 AM open.
  • Rope-drop the two biggest headliners: Stardust Racers in the Celestial Park hub and Battle at the Ministry in the Wizarding World. Open on one, hit the other immediately after.
  • Then Super Nintendo World (Mario Kart) and Isle of Berk — ride Hiccup's Wing Gliders in the morning.
  • Hit the low-capacity rides earlier than their small billing suggests: Constellation Carousel, Dragon Racer Rally, Mine Cart Madness.
  • Save Dark Universe for later but not last — Curse of the Werewolf builds a line through the afternoon.
  • Lunch in Celestial Park, the central hub. Stay until close; the park is at its best at night.
Battle at the Ministry breaks down often. Keep checking the app even if it reads closed for good — it comes back online with no warning.

Studios closes at 7 and IOA at 8, so build the day to finish at Islands of Adventure.

How the day runs

  • Morning — IOA at rope drop. Incredible Hulk Coaster and Hagrid's Magical Creatures Motorbike Adventure first; Hagrid's holds the longest line in the park. Then VelociCoaster, Forbidden Journey, and Skull Island: Reign of Kong.
  • Midday — Hogwarts Express from Hogsmeade to Diagon Alley. It's a different show in each direction and needs the park-to-park ticket.
  • Afternoon — Studios. Escape from Gringotts, Revenge of the Mummy, Hollywood Rip Ride Rockit, and the Minions rides.
  • Evening — back to IOA on the Hogwarts Express before Studios closes, then ride your favorites until the 8 PM close. Confirm the last Express departure; it stops running when Studios shuts.
Jurassic Park River Adventure is closed through Nov 19, 2026. Use the lockers at the other water rides instead. The Horror Make-Up Show at Studios is also likely still down.

Dinner at CityWalk

  • 1
    Toothsome Chocolate Emporium — steampunk-themed and known for absurd milkshakes and desserts. Open until 10:30 PM.
  • 2
    Bigfire — wood-fired steaks if you want a proper sit-down meal to close out the parks.

Ending here sets you up for the early start on Wednesday.

Atlantic sunrise → long haul

Ormond Beach, then west

Leave Orlando by 5:45–6:00 AM. Ormond Beach is about an hour east. Sunrise lands at 7:05 AM over the Atlantic — the one thing on this trip the Gulf side can't give you.

Where to go in Ormond & Daytona

  • 1
    Ormond / Daytona Beach — hard-packed sand you can actually drive onto. Park facing east and watch the sun come up.
  • 2
    Tomoka State Park — quiet riverfront trails, wildlife, and Timucuan history a few minutes from the beach. Open from 8 AM.
  • 3
    Daytona Boardwalk Amusements — classic oceanfront boardwalk, rides and arcade, open from 9 AM.
  • 4
    Daytona Aquarium & Rainforest Adventure — small but well done, with stingray feeding. A good air-conditioned break from midday heat.
Leave by 3:00 PM and drive straight through in shifts. Ormond to Irving is 1,050 miles and about 15.5 hours. Stopping at a hotel overnight would leave 9–10 hours for Thursday and put the 4 PM arrival at risk. Rotating three drivers is both cheaper and safer for the deadline.

Before you go

Tickets & setup

  • Buy a 3-Park Park-to-Park ticket. It's required for the Hogwarts Express and for moving between parks on the same day.
  • Express Pass pays off most at Epic Universe — more than at any other Universal park.
  • A Premier-tier hotel (Hard Rock, Portofino Bay, Royal Pacific) includes Express Pass Unlimited free for Studios and IOA, though usually not Epic Universe.
  • Download the Universal Orlando app and link tickets before you arrive — live waits, virtual queues, mobile food orders.

Hacks that actually move the needle

  • Rope-drop both park days. The first two hours beat any paid skip-the-line.
  • Two headliners first, then flex everything else around live wait times in the app.
  • Use single-rider lines on VelociCoaster, Hulk, and Rip Ride Rockit. With three of you, an hour wait becomes ten minutes.
  • Mobile-order lunch so you're not standing in a food line during peak ride hours.
