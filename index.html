Driving plan

Two legs of this trip are overnight drives. With three drivers nobody takes more than a four-hour stretch, and the Wednesday run home is the one to respect: it starts after a 6 AM wake-up and a full beach day, on top of five nights of short sleep. Each leg's rotation sits on its own day in the timeline.

Fri Sep 4 · departs 9:00 PM Irving → Destin 12 hrs · 690 mi · 3 shifts Driver rotation → Wed Sep 9 · departs 3:00 PM Ormond → Irving 15.5 hrs · 1,050 mi · 5 shifts Driver rotation →

Driver numbers are consistent across both legs, so whoever takes the 1 AM shift out is the same person on the 7 PM shift back. Decide who is Driver 1, 2, and 3 before you leave.

Conditions

Hurricanes

September is peak Atlantic hurricane season, the historical peak lands right around Sep 10. Check the National Hurricane Center in the days before you leave and have a Plan B in mind for the Destin leg if a system is tracking toward the Gulf.

Heat & storms

A daily afternoon thunderstorm in Orlando is normal for September, outdoor rides pause briefly and it clears fast. It's not a sign to change plans. Heat index will likely run mid-90s on the beach days and at the springs, so push water early rather than waiting until someone feels thirsty.

Tolls

None of this route needs them. I-20, I-59, I-65, I-10, I-75, and I-4 are all free interstates, including the exits for Universal. The only toll risk is I-4's separate Express managed lanes and, if your GPS gets creative, Florida's Turnpike or the Beachline (SR 528), none of which this route touches. Set your navigation app to avoid tolls before you leave so it doesn't reroute you into one.

Emerald Coast

Destin beach day

Arrive around 9-10 AM and go straight to the sand. Sugar-white beach, clear green-blue water. Do the bulk of your relaxing by mid-afternoon so the evening drive isn't rough on no sleep.

Where to go in Destin

  • 1
    Henderson Beach State Park — the cleanest, least crowded stretch of white sand in Destin. $6 per car. This is the one to settle in at for the day.
  • 2
    Destin Harbor Boardwalk — open 24 hrs. Shops, food, harbor views, and the launch point for dolphin cruises and jet ski rentals.
  • 3
    HarborWalk Village — lively waterfront hub for dinner and drinks. Parking runs about $20.
  • 4
    Gator Beach — free alligator shows and hold-a-baby-gator photos. Quick, odd, fun break from the beach.

Where to eat in Destin

  • 1
    The Back Porch — the original gulf-front seafood shack that started Destin's whole grilled amberjack tradition. Order right off the beach, no reservation needed.
  • 2
    Dewey Destin's Seafood — family-run dockside spot since 1976. Casual, on the water, honest seafood at fair prices.
  • 3
    AJ's Seafood & Oyster Bar — harbor views, oysters, and live music. Good pick if you want the HarborWalk energy with a real meal.
  • 4
    Boshamps Seafood & Oyster House — a step up in polish if you want a sit-down dinner before the evening drive.

Eat lunch on the beach, save a sit-down dinner for HarborWalk before you hit the road for Lake City.

Destin → Lake City · 3.5 hrs on I-10 · check in late evening

Saturday means beaches and beach-town lodging fill up. Book the Lake City hotel ahead of time. You'll roll in late, so Lake City is a highway stop, not a dining destination. Grab something quick near I-75 and crash.

Freshwater springs

Ginnie Springs, all day

Tubing the Santa Fe River, snorkeling, and swimming in water that sits at 72°F year-round. Privately owned, roughly $25 per adult, with tube, mask, and paddleboard rentals on site.

Good to know

A new alcohol and open-container policy took effect in April 2026, so expect it calmer than the party reputation it used to have. Bring water shoes and a dry bag.

If you'd rather have quiet over busy

  • A
    Ichetucknee Springs — widely called the best tubing run in Florida. State park, so more rules and fewer rentals, and it fills up on weekends.
  • B
    Gilchrist Blue Springs — right next door to Ginnie. Quieter, cheaper, excellent snorkeling.

Atlantic sunrise → long haul

Ormond Beach, then west

Leave Orlando by 5:45-6:00 AM. Ormond Beach is about an hour east. Sunrise lands at 7:05 AM over the Atlantic, the one thing on this trip the Gulf side can't give you.

Where to go in Ormond & Daytona

  • 1
    Ormond / Daytona Beach — hard-packed sand you can actually drive onto. Park facing east and watch the sun come up.
  • 2
    Tomoka State Park — quiet riverfront trails, wildlife, and Timucuan history a few minutes from the beach. Open from 8 AM.
  • 3
    Daytona Boardwalk Amusements — classic oceanfront boardwalk, rides and arcade, open from 9 AM.
  • 4
    Daytona Aquarium & Rainforest Adventure — small but well done, with stingray feeding. A good air-conditioned break from midday heat.

Where to eat in Ormond & Daytona

  • 1
    Aunt Catfish's on the River — riverfront breakfast and brunch spot, a local institution. Good call right after sunrise if you want a real sit-down meal before the beach.
  • 2
    Racing's North Turn Beach Bar & Grille — sits on the actual sand where NASCAR's beach races once ran. Casual, oceanfront, fun history with your lunch.
  • 3
    Down the Hatch — waterfront seafood dock at Ponce Inlet, a bit further south. Worth the drive if you have the time.
  • 4
    The Cellar — upscale option in a historic Ormond Beach house, if you want a proper meal before the long drive home instead of fast food on the highway.

Breakfast at Aunt Catfish's after sunrise, lunch beachside at Racing's North Turn, then eat something substantial before you leave at 3 PM. Nothing convenient on I-75 tonight.

Game plan

With Early Park Admission, be at the gate by 8:15 AM. Without it, 9:15 AM for the 10 AM open. The first two hours are everything. Eat before 11:30 AM or after 2:00 PM to dodge the lunch rush. Stay until close; the park is at its best at night.

Ride priority

    Ministry of Magic · Dark ride

    Best dark ride in the park. ~108 min avg wait, peaks at 140. Breaks down often, keep checking the app even if it shows closed.

    Rope dropSingle rider
  2. Stardust Racers
    Celestial Park · Dueling coaster

    Best thrill ride in the park. Dueling launches in both directions. If you don't open on Battle, open on this.

    Rope drop
  3. Monsters Unchained
    Dark Universe · Trackless dark ride

    Golden Ticket winner, Best Dark Ride 2025. Monster laboratory with practical sets. Lines build through the afternoon.

    Isle of Berk · Suspended coaster

    Best family coaster in the park. Swoops over the Viking village. Lines build through the day, ride it morning.

Day flow

Studios closes at 7 PM and IOA at 8 PM. Build the day to finish at Islands of Adventure. Start at IOA, hop to Studios via Hogwarts Express midday, then return to IOA for the evening.

Tickets & strategy

Tickets & setup

  • Buy a 3-Park Park-to-Park ticket. Required for the Hogwarts Express and for moving between parks on the same day.
  • No Express Pass, so arrival time is the whole strategy. Every ride order in this guide assumes it. Being at the gate for rope drop is doing the job a paid skip-the-line would otherwise do.
  • If you're staying at a Universal on-site hotel, Early Park Admission gets you in an hour before everyone else, the single biggest edge available without Express Pass.
  • Download the Universal Orlando app and link tickets before you arrive. Live waits, virtual queues, mobile food orders.

Hacks that move the needle

  • Rope-drop both park days. The first two hours beat any paid skip-the-line.
  • Two headliners first, then flex everything else around live wait times in the app.
  • Use single-rider lines on VelociCoaster, Hulk, Monsters Unchained, and Mine-Cart Madness. With three of you, an hour wait becomes ten minutes.
  • Mobile-order lunch so you're not standing in a food line during peak ride hours.
